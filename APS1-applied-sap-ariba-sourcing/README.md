**Current focus:** GICS01 Energy — OmniVerse Energy Ltd.

## Series scope

Study SAP Ariba Sourcing through sector-specific business decisions: understand the need, prepare supplier participation, design sourcing events, evaluate responses, defend awards, and trace the handoff to contracting and operational procurement.

## Start with Energy

[Open the Energy learning sprint](01-energy/README.md).

Use OmniVerse's canonical enterprise case and procurement blueprint. The first proposed practice scenario is selecting a maintenance-service supplier for a planned refinery shutdown, balancing safety, technical suitability, schedule, and whole-life value. New scenario details must be labelled illustrative assumptions.

## Learning approach

Explore business problems, procurement value streams and capabilities, requirements, SAP application responsibilities, data ownership, integration handoffs, controls, and practical sector scenarios. Build small pieces of evidence as learning progresses, with reflection and stakeholder simulation.

## Architecture alignment

Use TOGAF, BIZBOK, BABOK, DAMA-DMBOK, applicable ISO/IEC standards, and COBIT as relevant to the learning objectives. Distinguish business capabilities, processes, application functions, data, integration, and sector domain rules.

## Learning artifacts

Each sector folder is a home for future sprint sessions, architecture models, applied exercises, case-study deliverables, and assessments. Energy contains the starting brief; the remaining sectors are scoped landing pages. No tenant configuration or completed implementation is claimed.
