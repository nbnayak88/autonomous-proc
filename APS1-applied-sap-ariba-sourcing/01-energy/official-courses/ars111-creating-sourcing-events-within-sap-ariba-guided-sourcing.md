## Proposed Energy experiment

Design an illustrative refinery-service RFP with qualification gates, questions, lots and line items.

Use fictional OmniVerse data. Capture requirements, accountable roles, data ownership, application boundaries, controls and review/test evidence. These are proposed learner exercises, not completed implementations or legal contract advice.
