**APS1-applied-sap-ariba-sourcing / Energy / Direct-material sourcing**

Use the Product Sourcing dashboard to explore direct-material events.

A critical spare or expensive asset is not automatically direct material. Confirm business usage, category policy and Product Sourcing fit; the sourcing platform does not replace ERP inventory or supply planning.

## Proposed Energy experiment

Assess a suitable process-input or material sourcing scenario; justify direct-spend classification before designing the event.

Use fictional OmniVerse data. Define requirements, accountable roles, data ownership, application boundaries, controls and expected review/test evidence. No completed tenant configuration, supplier award or realized benefit is asserted.
