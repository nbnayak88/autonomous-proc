## Scope and boundary

Use built-in procurement reports, filters and interpretation.

This course teaches native SAP Ariba Procurement reporting. Analytics pathways can reference it; it does not teach SAP Analytics Cloud, Databricks or Spend Control Tower implementation.

## Proposed Energy experiment

Define a report for overdue refinery requisitions and open purchase orders, with data owner and action.

Use fictional OmniVerse data. Define the business requirement, data owner, application responsibility, control and expected test evidence. No real tenant change or achieved outcome is claimed.
