## SuccessLabs learning progression — recommendation

| Stage | Courses | Proposed Energy evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Understand purpose and navigation | ARS010, ARS109 | Explain sourcing responsibilities and demonstrate a buyer navigation checklist |
| Build the sourcing project and event | ARS110, ARS111 | Project brief, roles, tasks, qualification gates and draft RFx content |
| Compare and defend decisions | ARS112, ARS113, ARS114 | Pricing assumptions, evaluation model, award rationale and sourcing report |
| Explore direct-material fit | ARS222, ARS345 | Justified category choice, Product Sourcing scope and administration/readiness checks |
| Administer reusable sourcing behavior | ARX280, ARX290, ARS280 | Template governance, access/data ownership and tested event-template rules |

These are applied learning stages, not additional mandatory SAP prerequisite chains or fixed SuccessLabs level awards.

## Energy learning thread

Use the [canonical OmniVerse case](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/tree/main/01-energy/omniverse-energy-ltd). The initial proposed exercise is a refinery sourcing project that connects requirements, supplier qualification, event design, pricing options, evaluation, award rationale and reporting.

Keep all invented suppliers, quantities, prices and dates labelled illustrative. Requirements, data ownership, approval decisions and integration handoffs should be traceable. An award recommendation does not authorize hazardous work or establish realized savings.

Primary lab intent: **05 Build / IIL**, supported as appropriate by Discover, Flow, Design, Validate and Govern. Use TOGAF, BIZBOK, BABOK, DAMA-DMBOK, applicable ISO/IEC guidance and COBIT where helpful; formal compliance is not asserted.
