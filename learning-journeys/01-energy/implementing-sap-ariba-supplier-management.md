## Energy evidence and application boundaries

Use a fictional equipment or maintenance supplier to connect identity/contact quality, registration evidence, category/region qualification, preferred status, performance and risk decisions.

Registration, qualification, preferred status, performance and risk are separate concepts. A risk score does not replace technical qualification or operational safety authorization. ERP supplier identity, Ariba profile identity and Business Network relationships require explicit ownership and reconciliation.

[Official supplier-management capability page](https://learning.sap.com/products/intelligent-spend-management/ariba/supplier-management) provides portfolio context; it is not an additional course.

Proposed learner evidence: requirements, accountable roles, data ownership, application responsibilities, approvals, configuration decisions and tests. Use [canonical OmniVerse facts](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/tree/main/01-energy/omniverse-energy-ltd); mark new suppliers, dates and scores as illustrative. Apply TOGAF, BIZBOK, BABOK, DAMA-DMBOK, relevant ISO/IEC guidance and COBIT as useful references without claiming formal compliance.
