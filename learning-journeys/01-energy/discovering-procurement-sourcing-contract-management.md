## Prerequisites and learning order

SAP lists **basic knowledge of procurement processes in SAP Ariba and SAP Cloud ERP** as the parent journey prerequisite. Course-specific requirements must also be considered.

| Course | SAP stated prerequisite(s) | Planning implication |
|---|---|---|
| S45210 | S45000 | Learn ERP procurement before ERP source determination |
| S45200 | S45000 | Learn ERP procurement before ERP purchasing agreement management |
| ARS602 | ARS060 — SAP Ariba Contracts Overview; ARS601 | An additional prerequisite sits outside this 15-course inventory; link it rather than claiming it is included |
| ARS640 | ARS601 | Understand workspaces before assembled-document authoring |
| ARS641 | ARS640 | Learn document authoring before clause-library administration |
| ARS644 | ARS601; ARS602; ARX280 | Generic template administration appears later in SAP's displayed list, but is required before this contract-specific course |

[ARS060 — SAP Ariba Contracts Overview](https://learning.sap.com/courses/sap-ariba-contracts-overview) is a referenced prerequisite, not a sixteenth course in this journey.

SAP lists no prerequisites on S4A010, ARS110–113, ARS601 and ARS642. The reviewed S45000 and ARX280 pages do not show a prerequisite section. The study sequence below is our recommendation; do not confuse it with SAP's published course order.

1. Start with S4A010 and clarify cross-platform responsibilities.
2. Learn S45000 before S45210 and S45200.
3. Follow Guided Sourcing: ARS110 → ARS111 → ARS112 → ARS113.
4. Review ARS060 as needed, then ARS601 → ARS602 and ARS601 → ARS640 → ARS641.
5. Add ARS642 for legacy migration; complete ARX280 before ARS644 alongside the required contract prerequisites.
6. Assemble an Energy source-to-contract handoff and review whether evidence supports each decision.

## Application boundaries to preserve

| Responsibility | Learning home |
|---|---|
| Competitive sourcing projects, supplier responses and award decisions | APS1 / ARS110–113 |
| Ariba contract workspaces, assembled documents, clauses and amendments | ASN3 / ARS601–644 |
| Purchasing info records, source lists, quota arrangements and ERP source determination | ERP / S45210 |
| S/4HANA purchasing contracts, scheduling agreements and related analytics | ERP / S45200 |
| Operational contract compliance inside Ariba Buying & Invoicing | [Existing APO5 Energy journey](../../APO5-applied-sap-ariba-buying-invoicing-fieldglass/01-energy/README.md), ARP300/301 |
| Reusable project templates across strategic sourcing solutions | Shared ARX280; link from Sourcing, Contracts and Supplier Management |
| Technical interface design | [AIG2 Energy integration](../../AIG2-applied-sap-integration-suite-procurement/01-energy/README.md); cross-reference existing integration courses |

S/4HANA source determination is not an Ariba auction. A signed Ariba contract workspace is not itself the same object as an ERP purchasing agreement or an Ariba Buying compliance contract. Define object ownership and handoffs explicitly.

## Energy learning thread

Use [OmniVerse's canonical case](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/tree/main/01-energy/omniverse-energy-ltd). Proposed exercise:

**Define a refinery supply/service requirement → organize a sourcing project → evaluate qualified supplier offers → justify an award → establish a controlled contract workspace → identify how agreed commercial terms reach the operational purchasing system.**

This is an illustrative learning extension. Preserve canonical enterprise facts and label invented suppliers, prices, quantities and dates. Learner evidence should connect business requirements, data ownership, application responsibilities, approvals, integration, tests and benefits measures. Legal and operational safety approvals require accountable specialists.

Apply TOGAF, BIZBOK, BABOK, DAMA-DMBOK, relevant ISO/IEC guidance and COBIT as useful references; no certification or formal compliance is asserted.
